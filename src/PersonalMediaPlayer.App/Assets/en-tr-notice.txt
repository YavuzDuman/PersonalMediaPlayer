English–Turkish hover glosses in en-tr.json are generated from two lexicons.

Wiktionary English–Turkish translations (2015-01-02 extract by Matthias Buchmeier),
licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 and the GNU Free Documentation License.
https://en.wiktionary.org/

FreeDict eng-tur, from Mehmet Ali Vardar's gtksozluk2, licensed under the GNU GPL.
https://github.com/freedict/fd-dictionaries/tree/master/eng-tur

Closed-class contractions (don't, I'm, and similar) are resolved in code.
Content words come from the lexicon file.
Multi-word phrases in en-tr-phrases.json come from the same two lexicons.
