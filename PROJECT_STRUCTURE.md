# Project structure

The current layout and storage model are described in [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md).

How to use the app is in [docs/guide.md](docs/guide.md).

Tests live in `tests/PersonalMediaPlayer.Tests`. They compile the app sources they cover and check download output, stream checks, the browser handoff, playlist listing, captions, saved words, resume positions, home search, and the in-memory search session.

The shell opens on Home. The other pages are Library, Playlists, Saved words, Recordings, Download, Merge, Capture, and Settings. Home search looks through library file names, captions already stored for those videos, and online videos saved in playlists. A caption row shows the video name, the time, and the line, and opens the player at that moment. A result shows a thumbnail, or a video or photo icon, the name, and Video or Photo. A library file keeps the Library label. A local video that is also in playlists stays one row and lists those names. A photo opens as a photo. Coming back keeps that search, and the searches on Library, Playlists, and Saved words, until the app closes. Library also keeps the album that was open. Home headings use All, beside the title, to open the full list. A playlist on Home is a cover of up to four videos. Play on the cover starts the first one that can be opened, and the card opens that playlist. Hovering previews the videos. On the Playlists page, search filters by name, and drag or sort saves the order. In the player, shuffle plays that list in a random order, once each, and repeat can replay one video or the whole list. The saved order stays as it is. Play next puts a chosen video first in the queue, and Add to queue adds it at the end. Dragging reorders that waiting list. The current video keeps playing. Clear removes every waiting video, and Undo puts that list or the last removal back in the same order. The queue plays before shuffle and repeat, for a library file or an online page. That waiting list is saved in `play-queue.json` and comes back in the same order after the app opens, including videos placed first with Play next. Opening the app does not start playback. The open video is saved in `watching.json` with its position and comes back paused in the small player. Returning to the full player shows the waiting queue with it. Close on the player removes that video and leaves the queue. A missing file stays and says Missing. The undo is not kept. Both files are separate from playlists and the download queue, and they are not part of a backup. Chapters from a local file or an online page are listed beside the player when that video has them. Chapters shows or hides that list while the app is open. A video with no chapters hides the button and the list. The choice and the list are not saved, and bookmarks stay as they are. Undo restores the last removal, move, or sort. A finished playlist video is marked watched, and a right-click can change that mark. The player panel shows a picture on each row and can reorder the list while a video plays, and undo there does not start the video again. Closing that list leaves a card on the video. Opening a video that is not in that playlist hides the card and the Playlist button together. Finished downloads can be saved one at a time, or several ready files can be saved into one folder. A file already in that folder asks before it is replaced, and one failure leaves that file ready. The player stays over the other pages. Leaving it keeps playback in a small player at the bottom right, with play, pause, volume, close, and a return to the full player. Closing the app while a video is open restores that video paused in the same small player. That volume is shared with the full player. A download preview keeps a separate volume. The full player is opaque so the page behind it does not show through the video name. Switching those two views does not start the video again. Opening a different video stops the previous one. **Words** opens the saved-words list beside the video and hides it completely. Import can copy a file into the library or link it in place through `Library/links.json`. **Connect folder** watches a PC folder and its subfolders, links new photos and videos in place, and lists each path once. New photos and videos show up while the app is open, including the files inside a folder that is moved in. A connected folder that is missing when the app opens is watched again when it comes back. Refresh scans that folder again. A missing file stays listed. **Locate in folder** reviews matches for the selected missing files before `RelocateLink` runs, and asks when more than one file could fit. **Locate** still picks one file. Disconnect removes only the watch entry in `Library/connected-folders.json`. That watch list stays on this PC. Library can select several files and add them to a playlist or to favorites together. The browser extension is the unpacked package in `extension/` and is left out of `PersonalMediaPlayer.slnx`. Settings can export and restore a backup of playlists, saved words, bookmarks, playback positions, favorites, library links, and settings. Export replaces the chosen file only after the temporary backup is complete. Restore checks the records, asks first, and keeps the previous copy as `recovery.pmpbackup`. Media files stay out of that backup. The list of connected folders stays on this PC.

Merge, the video editor, the shared playback bar, stream playback, and the subtitle hover list live under `src/PersonalMediaPlayer.App`.
